Exclusive Features

The definitive MMD rendering experience, re-architected from the ground up.

Temporal Anti-Aliasing

Improved edge anti-aliasing and temporal stability to reduce subpixel flickering, providing a stable image on complex meshes.

Screen Space Voxel GI

Hybrid GI architecture combining voxel grids with screen-space cone tracing for real-time indirect lighting without NaN artifacts.

Hybrid HBAO / SSDO

Horizon-based ambient occlusion and directional occlusion for accurate contact shading and material definition.

Percentage-Closer Soft Shadows

Distance-based soft shadows that blur based on the distance from the caster, reducing harsh shadow edges.

Optimized SSR

Upgraded Screen Space Reflections using Binary Search and depth-aware blur for improved performance and accuracy.

Contact Shadows

Screen-space raymarched shadows to reduce light leaking and grounding issues, improving spatial grounding.
