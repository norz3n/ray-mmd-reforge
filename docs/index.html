Exclusive Features

The definitive MMD rendering experience, re-architected from the ground up.

Unified Hi-Z Core

One hierarchical Z-buffer shared by SSR, SSGI, HBAO/SSDO, contact shadows, and glass refraction — academically correct DDA traversal with near-plane clamping and cell-boundary fixes.

Screen Space Global Illumination

Modular SSGI with progressive Hi-Z LOD gather, multi-bounce interreflection, rough specular GI, and visibility masking — replacing legacy VXGI entirely.

Hybrid HBAO / SSDO

Horizon-based ambient occlusion and directional occlusion with multi-scale Hi-Z sampling, plus occlusion-aware bent normals for believable indirect shading.

PCSS & Variance Shadow Maps

Percentage-closer soft shadows with variable penumbra for local lights, and grain-free high-resolution VSM sun shadows that stay clean on character faces.

PBR Screen-Space Reflections

Clean-room Hi-Z ray traversal with binary-search refinement, depth-aware bilateral blur, edge fade, and energy-conserving BRDF integration.

AgX Tone Mapping

Exact 6th-order polynomial implementation of the official Blender 4.0 AgX mapper (default), with an ACES-fitted option for a filmic, highlight-preserving look.

Temporal Anti-Aliasing

5-tap Catmull-Rom bicubic history reconstruction, Karis luma weighting, variance clipping, and depth-validated history for a stable, flicker-free image.

Physical Skin, Hair & Eyes

Dynamic-curvature subsurface scattering with scale-invariant thickness, procedural anisotropic hair with Kajiya-Kay highlights, and corneal-dome procedural eyes.

Glass & Transparency Pipeline

Chromatic-dispersion refraction traced against the scene Hi-Z, tinted glass presets inheriting MMD diffuse color, and forced-transparency material presets.

Cinematic Post-Processing

Clean-room hexagonal bokeh DOF with blade control, anti-firefly Karis bloom, FidelityFX CAS sharpening, anamorphic flares, and dithered volumetrics.

Procedural Foliage & Cloth

Vertex wind animation engine with four vegetation presets, rewritten cloth BRDF with Charlie Sheen distribution, and thin-film iridescence for advanced surfaces.
